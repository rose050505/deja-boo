Paste code. The same matcher the CLI and the Kiro hook use runs here, in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

This page only checks against the sample graveyards. For your own repository, use the CLI: npx -y github:rose050505/deja-boo index in its root, then check (setup).

Every warning shows the commit, the date, the deleted lines and the cause of death verbatim from the commit message. When history gives no reason, it says so. No server, no account: this page is static.
